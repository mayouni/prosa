# Self-reading of the draft, for the author

This is the drafter's own reading of each page against `checklists/chapter.md` and the voice reader's brief, made once, on 2026-11-26. For each page it quotes the passage judged most his and the passage judged least his, so that he can go straight to the weak ones. The drafter wrote the pages, so this is no fresh reading: the reading that the house style declares (`voice_reader`) is due for every chapter.

## `00-preface.md`

**Most his.** "Two remarks, each made before, each made again. This book is about the remedy."

Why: the long sentence, then the verdict, resting only on the record.

**Least his.** "A correction becomes a rule, the rule carries the remark that created it, and from that day the court raises it: I do not."

Why: a product sentence in the cadence of the proposal; the preface is his to write last (C9), and this page is a frame around five holes.
