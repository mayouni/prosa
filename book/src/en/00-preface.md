This preface is the page I write last, from the book that exists. This draft holds its frame: what the record shows about why the book was written, and the places where only I can speak.

[AUTHOR: the opening. The day you decided that your corrections to AI agents had to become a language, and what you were looking at on the screen that day.]

## What the record shows

I write books and sites with AI agents, and I correct them. The corrections are written down, with their dates, because my house keeps its record.

On 5 November 2026 I looked at a page of the Zayn site and wrote: "avoid the narrative, succinct, non declarative style of writing". The file that keeps this remark, `zayn/doc/design/WRITING.md`, adds that I had asked for it more than once, and gives the reason in one sentence: "It drifted back each time because it lived only in a promise."

On 8 November 2026 I wrote, about pages that listed what my project lacked: "Generalise this issue on all Softanza project sistes because I raised this before but you seem to persist on it". The typing is mine, kept as typed.

Two remarks, each made before, each made again. This book is about the remedy.

[AUTHOR: what repeating a correction costs you. The record holds the words; it does not hold the fatigue, or what you did after the second time.]

## What the book is

Prosa is a language in which a writing style is declared, and a program judges every text against the declaration. I call that program the court. A correction becomes a rule, the rule carries the remark that created it, and from that day the court raises it: I do not.

The book has ten chapters. The first two say why a style drifts and what the field built against it. Chapters 3 to 8 teach the language at the keyboard, one kind of declaration at a time. Chapters 9 and 10 put an AI agent under the court, then a second house.

[AUTHOR: who you wrote it for. A proposal: the author who writes with AI agents and wants the page to stay his; the editor of a house; the programmer who builds the tools; the AI agent that reads the book as data.]

## How it was written

This book was drafted under its own court. A file named `book.proz` stands beside the chapters and declares what each one must be: a title of six words at most that names its subject, a first sentence that says what the subject is, the words of my house, the measure of my voice. The court judged every chapter, and its first verdict on each was kept before any repair. The table is in `research/trial/`: it counts what an AI agent still gets wrong with my rules in its prompt.

Every terminal block in the book is pasted from a run. Where a page shows `prosa check`, the command was `node tools/prosa/cli.js check`, the reference court at version 0.1.0.

And every particular that only I know is marked as a hole until I fill it. You have met three on this page already.
