The five commitments of Prosa

The commitments of Prosa are the five principles that decide what the language declares and what its court refuses. They ensure that a style stays the author's own: each rule comes from a ruling or a measure, and the court holds every text to it.

Commitment 1: a correction is made once

Each remark of the author becomes a rule with a stable code, a refused example, a written example and the remark itself as evidence. From that day the court raises the remark on every text, and the author rules on new matters.

The rule below is the affirmative rule of the public pages of AYOUNI Press, the first house. Its EVIDENCE field quotes the ruling that created it.

DEFINE PATTERN lack_listed (  MATCHES [    "not yet",    ["is not", "are not", "was not", "were not", "has not", "have not", "had not",     "isn't", "aren't", "wasn't", "weren't", "hasn't", "haven't", "hadn't"] WITHIN 7 "yet",    [nobody, "no one"] [has, had, have],    "never" [been, run, measured, shown, tested, perceived],    ["has not", "have not", "had not", "hasn't", "haven't", "hadn't"] [been, measured, shown, tested, run],    ["Limit:", "Limits:"],    ["not", "not been"] [measured, shown, tested, perceived],    ["not run", "not been run"],    [unmeasured, untested, unproven],    "yet to",    ["nothing runs", "nothing is measured"]  ],  KEEPS ["do not run", "to not run", "must not run", "should not run", "can not run", "will not run", "don't not run"],  IN [title, heading, lede, body, list],  LANGUAGE en,  CODE LACK_LISTED, SEVERITY error,  SAYS "The line lists a lack. Commitment 2: a voice is a measure taken from the author's pages

The reference book of the house gives a profile: a median sentence of 16 words, 15 colons and 0.5 dashes per thousand words. Prosa holds a text to that distribution in both directions.

  • An upper bound, AT_MOST, refuses noise: the long sentence, the dash, the cold exclamation.
  • A lower bound, AT_LEAST, asks for what makes the voice present: the short verdict and the colon.

A court that only forbids produces a disciplined and colder text. The lower bounds keep the warmth of the measured pages in the drafts that follow them.

Commitment 3: a style is held to itself

A rule binds a text only when the style adopts it, so the same pattern is noise in one house and voice in another. The author of the first house writes: "The bottleneck of our age is not writing. It is admission." A generic list of AI habits refuses that sentence, and his own style keeps it.

A style takes the rules of another with ADOPTS and leaves one out with DECLINES. The public style of the first house adopts its book style and adds one rule:

DEFINE STYLE ayouni_public (  ADOPTS [ayouni],  HOLDS [lack_listed, manque_liste])

Commitment 4: a rule enters on evidence

Evidence is a dated ruling of the author or a measure on a named corpus. The court refuses a rule that carries none, under the code RULE_WITHOUT_EVIDENCE: fashion has no way into a style.

Commitment 5: undecidable is silence

The court decides words, patterns, positions and counts, and it raises a finding only where the specification makes the answer the same on every machine. Whether a sentence is a riddle, or whether a paragraph sounds like its author, belongs to a reading by the author or by an AI model under a declared question.

Prosa names the reading, records its answer and keeps it apart from the verdict. A reading carries no code and leaves the exit code of the court unchanged.

DEFINE READING voice_reader (  ON chapter,  ASKS "Which three passages are most him, and which one is least him?",  RECORDS "research/voice")

The five effects for a writer who works with AI models

  • The author stops repeating himself: a remark costs one rule.
  • The voice stays the author's: the measure comes from his pages, so a draft is pulled toward him and away from the average.
  • A style becomes an asset: it is versioned, tested by fixtures, reviewed in a pull request and shared between books, sites and posts.
  • Every refusal explains itself: it names the rule, shows the written form and cites the ruling, so a new collaborator or a new AI agent learns the house from its diagnostics.
  • The house states its style to others: a publisher, a documentation team or a brand writes its own .proz file and holds every contributor, a person or an AI agent, to the same law.
