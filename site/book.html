The book: Prose Under Law

The book of Prosa carries the working title "Prose Under Law": it teaches the language to a writer who works with AI models. It lets a reader go from a first remark on a draft to a style the court keeps on every page.

What the manuscript holds and how it is governed

The manuscript opens on a style held by a promise and on the reason it drifts back, then teaches the ten declarations one at a time. Its chapters are Markdown files, and a declaration, book.proz, binds them to the style of AYOUNI Press: the court of Prosa judges every chapter of the manuscript against the law the chapters teach.

The chapters, their examples and the declaration stand in the folder of the book.

Prosa is a Softanza project. This site is governed by its own declaration, site.proz, and judged by the court it describes.
