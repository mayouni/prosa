The diagnostics of the court

A diagnostic of Prosa is a finding of the court: it names the file and the line, the code, the rule and the ruling the rule comes from. It lets the user, a person or an AI agent, repair a text by its code and learn the house from each refusal.

On this page

  • The eight parts of a finding
  • The 31 codes the court raises by itself, by level
  • The codes a style declares for its own rules

The eight parts of a finding

A finding has six parts in the envelope C2 of the Softanza family: code, severity, message, span, cites and language. The Prosa court adds level and subject. The option --json prints the findings as data:

$ prosa check conformance/fixtures/term-synonym.proz --json{ "diagnostics": [  {   "code": "TERM_SYNONYM",   "severity": "error",   "message": "\"developer\" stands for the person who writes programs: the house word is \"programmer\".",   "span": {    "file": "conformance/fixtures/term-synonym.md",    "line": 3   },   "cites": [    "rule:programmer",    "evidence:The author, 2026-11-26: a ruling written for this fixture"   ],   "language": "prosa",   "level": 3,   "subject": "programmer"  } ]}

A code is a stable UPPER_SNAKE name, and a code is used for one meaning for the whole life of the language. The field cites holds the name of the rule and its evidence.

The 31 codes the court raises by itself, by level

The tables below are written at build time from conformance/violations.json, contract 0.1: the page and the court read the same file.

Level 1: the syntax of a .proz file

CodeSeverityWhat the court saysThe invariant
UNEXPECTED_TOKENerrorUnexpected {token}.A .proz file is a sequence of USE lines and DEFINE declarations.
UNTERMINATED_STRINGerrorA quoted string is not closed on its line.A string opens and closes on one line.
UNKNOWN_KINDerror{kind} is not a kind of declaration.The kinds are closed: STYLE, TERM, WORDLIST, PATTERN, PROFILE, FORM, ZONE, PIECE, ALLOW, READING.
UNKNOWN_FIELDerror{field} is not a field of {kind}.Each kind has a closed list of fields.
DUPLICATE_FIELDerrorThe field {field} stands twice.A field stands once in a declaration.
DUPLICATE_DECLARATIONerrorThe name {name} is already declared at line {first}.A name is declared once across all kinds.
MISSING_FIELDerror{kind} needs the field {field}.A declaration carries its required fields.
BAD_VALUEerrorThe value of {field} is not lawful: {why}.A field's value has the shape its kind declares.

Level 2: the declaration

CodeSeverityWhat the court saysThe invariant
RULE_WITHOUT_EVIDENCEerrorThis {kind} carries no EVIDENCE: a rule enters on a dated ruling or a measure.Every rule cites the ruling or the measure it comes from.
UNKNOWN_REFERENCEerror{field} names {name}, which is not a declared {kinds}.A reference names a declaration of the right kind.
ADOPTION_CYCLEerror{field} leads back to this declaration.Adoption and extension form no cycle.
TERM_REFUSED_AND_REQUIREDerrorThe style {style} asks for "{word}" and refuses it in {rule}.A style never refuses the word it asks for.
EXAMPLE_NOT_REFUSEDerrorThe REFUSED example is not refused by its own pattern: "{text}".A pattern refuses its own refused example.
EXAMPLE_NOT_LAWFULerrorThe WRITTEN example is refused by its own pattern: "{text}".A pattern accepts its own written example.
PIECE_WITHOUT_SUBJECTerrorThe form {form} places the subject, and the piece declares no SUBJECT.A piece whose form places the subject declares it.
BOUND_INVERTEDerrorThe bound of {field} has its lower end above its upper end.A bound's lower end is at most its upper end.

Level 3: the text

CodeSeverityWhat the court saysThe invariant
TITLE_TOO_LONGerrorThe title holds {n} words; the form allows {max}.A title names its subject in few words.
TITLE_MISSINGerrorThe piece has no title.A governed text has a title.
TITLE_WITHOUT_SUBJECTerrorThe title does not name the subject "{subject}".A title names its subject.
LEDE_WITHOUT_SUBJECTerrorThe first sentence does not name the subject "{subject}".The first sentence names the subject and says what it is.
SUBJECT_NOT_FOUNDerrorThe piece takes its subject from the front matter, and the page declares none.A page declares its subject.
SAY_ONCEerror"{pair}" stands twice in the first {limit} words (first at line {first}).A statement is made once per screen.
HEADING_MISSINGerrorThe form's heading "{heading}" is missing.A form's fixed headings are all present.
HEADING_OUT_OF_ORDERerrorThe heading "{heading}" stands before a heading the form places ahead of it.A form's fixed headings keep their order.
HEADING_NOT_SENTENCE_CASEwarningThe heading "{heading}" capitalizes every word.A heading is written in sentence case.
CLOSES_ON_SUMMARYerrorThe text closes on "{phrase}".A text closes on something new for its reader.
HOLE_OPENerrorA hole is still open at status {status}: {hole}A hole is closed by the author before the text is reviewed.
ALLOW_STALEerrorThis ALLOW excuses {code} on a line holding "{holds}", and no such finding stands.An excuse lives as long as the line it excuses.
PIECE_FILE_NOT_FOUNDerrorThe piece names {path}, and no such file stands.A piece names a text that exists.
TERM_SYNONYMerrorA refused word stands for a declared term.One thing has one word.

Level 4: the measure

CodeSeverityWhat the court saysThe invariant
OFF_PROFILEwarningA measure of the text stands outside the declared profile.A text keeps the measure of its voice.

The codes a style declares for its own rules

A TERM, a WORDLIST, a PATTERN or a PROFILE raises the code its declaration names. TERM_SYNONYM and OFF_PROFILE are the defaults of TERM and PROFILE. The style of the first house declares these codes:

  • FORBIDDEN_WORD refuses a word of the forbidden list and shows the written form.
  • CALQUE refuses a calque or a homophone and shows the intended word.
  • INTENSIFIER, HEDGE and SUPERLATIVE warn on the three lists of noise.
  • EMOJI_IN_TEXT refuses an emoji in text a reader sees.
  • LACK_LISTED refuses a line of a public page that lists a lack.
  • VOICE_ABSENT warns when a long text stands below the lower bounds of the voice.

The constitution declares PLACEHOLDER_LEFT: a placeholder that stands where a fact belongs is refused in every governed text.
