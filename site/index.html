A Softanza language for written text

Prosa: a correction is made once.

Prosa is a declarative language that governs written text by compilation: a writing style is declared in .proz files, and a court refuses, by name, every sentence the declaration forbids. It ensures that a remark an author makes about a draft becomes a rule the court raises on every later draft.

The three layers of a governed text

The prose stays in Markdown or HTML, and a .proz file beside it declares what the prose must be. A declaration has three layers. Each lower layer constrains the one above it.

  • The reader's rights are a small constitution shipped with the language: a title names its subject, a statement stands once per screen, and a placeholder left in place of a fact is refused.
  • The house style is the declared voice of a publisher, a team or one author: its terms, its word lists, its patterns, its profile and its forms.
  • The piece binds one text, or a set of files, to a style, a form and a subject term.

The user who writes under a declaration is a person or an AI agent. The court judges both by the same law.

Why a style written in a prompt drifts back

A style that lives in a promise drifts back: the author makes the same remark on the next draft. The cause is measured. An AI model writes toward the average of what it read, and an instruction in a prompt is one weak signal against that average.

  • The pull is statistical: instruction-tuned AI models use present participial clauses two to five times more often than people do, and nominalizations one and a half to two times more often (Reinhart et al., PNAS 2025 ↗).
  • The pull reaches published science: at least 13.5 % of the biomedical abstracts of 2024 carry the excess vocabulary of AI models (Kobak et al., Science Advances 2025 ↗).
  • The pull flattens voices: essays written with an instruction-tuned AI model are closer to each other than essays written alone (Padmakumar and He, ICLR 2024 ↗), and AI suggestions move Indian writers toward Western styles (Agarwal, Naaman and Vashistha, CHI 2025 ↗).
  • A prohibition in a prompt is unreliable: on one open AI model, naming a forbidden word can raise its chance of appearing (Rana, 2026 preprint ↗), and negated prompts are a documented weakness of AI models (Jang et al., 2022 ↗).

Prosa moves the law out of the prompt and into a court that sits outside the AI model: a defect is refused before the text reaches a reader.

A declaration, a text and the verdict of the court

The declaration below gives one thing one word: the person who writes programs is a programmer. The text under it uses another word. The court refuses the line.

The declaration: term-synonym.proz
// Expected: TERM_SYNONYMDEFINE STYLE house (  HOLDS [programmer])DEFINE TERM programmer (  MEANS "the person who writes programs",  REFUSES [developer, developers],  MENTIONS skipped,  EVIDENCE "The author, 2026-11-26: a ruling written for this fixture")DEFINE PIECE the_page (  FILE "term-synonym.md",  FOLLOWS house)
The text: term-synonym.md
# The court of ProsaA developer writes the rule once.
The verdict
$ prosa check conformance/fixtures/term-synonym.prozconformance/fixtures/term-synonym.md:3  error  TERM_SYNONYM  programmer    "developer" stands for the person who writes programs: the house word is "programmer".    Rule programmer · The author, 2026-11-26: a ruling written for this fixture1 error(s), 0 warning(s) across 2 file(s) at level 4

The verdict names the file and the line, the code, the refused word, the house word, the rule and the ruling the rule comes from. The same text gives the same verdict on every machine: the court uses no AI model to judge words, positions and measures.

The figures of the reference court

The reference court is version 0.1.0 of language 0.1. It is one JavaScript file that runs under Node and in a browser.

  • The conformance suite holds 66 fixtures, each a refused case or its lawful twin, and the court passes 66 of 66.
  • The contract of diagnostics lists 31 codes the court raises by itself, each with its level and its invariant.
  • On 81 pages of three AYOUNI Press books in English and 42 pages in French, the twelve measures of the court equal those of the house lint on every page.
