The ten kinds of declaration

A kind is one of the ten forms a declaration of Prosa takes in a .proz file. The kinds let a house state its whole style as data: its rules, the structure of its texts, its excuses and the judgments that stay with a reader.

The grammar every declaration shares

A declaration is written DEFINE KIND name ( FIELD value, ... ). A keyword is UPPER_SNAKE, a name is lower_snake, a string stands between two double quotes on one line, and // opens a comment. The list of kinds is closed, and each one has a closed list of fields.

A USE "path.proz" line reads another file, and USE "prosa:reader_rights" reads the constitution shipped with the language. A name is declared once across all the files read together.

KindRoleWhat it declares
STYLEGroups rulesThe voice of a house, or a set a house adopts
TERMRuleThe house word for one thing, and the words refused in its place
WORDLISTRuleA list of refused words or phrases, with an optional budget
PATTERNRuleA refused construction, at a position when one is given

ALLOWExcuseOne line of one file excused from one code, with a reason

READINGJudgment of a readerA question put to a reader, and the folder of its records

STYLE: a named set of rules

A STYLE holds rules, adopts other styles and declines the rules it leaves out. A rule binds a text only through a style: a rule that stands outside every style judges nothing. The public style of AYOUNI Press adopts the book style and holds the affirmative rule.

DEFINE STYLE ayouni_public (  ADOPTS [ayouni],  HOLDS [lack_listed, manque_liste])

TERM: one word for one thing

A TERM names the house word and refuses the others. With MENTIONS skipped, a short quotation of the refused word is a mention, and the court leaves it unjudged.

DEFINE TERM programmer (  MEANS "the person who writes programs",  REFUSES [developer, developers],  LANGUAGE en, MENTIONS skipped,  EVIDENCE "FRAMEWORK.md section 4: developer for programmer is a forbidden word")

WORDLIST: a list of refused words

A WORDLIST refuses each entry of its list, and the field SAYS gives the sentence the diagnostic shows. A list with a budget, AT_MOST 4 PER_THOUSAND, is judged as one measure over the whole piece.

DEFINE WORDLIST hedges (  REFUSES ["maybe, and probably", somehow, "kind of", "sort of", "a little bit", "more or less", "in a way", "I guess",           "I think that", "it seems that", "probably, and"],  LANGUAGE en,  CODE HEDGE, SEVERITY warning,  SAYS "State the claim at its true size.",  EVIDENCE "FRAMEWORK.md section 4, stacked hedges; checklists/noise.md")

PATTERN: a refused construction with its own test

A PATTERN is written over words: a row of words, a group in brackets, a gap with WITHIN, and a position with AT. Its REFUSED and WRITTEN examples are the test of the rule: the court refuses a pattern that accepts its refused example or refuses its written one.

DEFINE PATTERN lack_listed (  MATCHES [    "not yet",    ["is not", "are not", "was not", "were not", "has not", "have not", "had not",     "isn't", "aren't", "wasn't", "weren't", "hasn't", "haven't", "hadn't"] WITHIN 7 "yet",    [nobody, "no one"] [has, had, have],    "never" [been, run, measured, shown, tested, perceived],    ["has not", "have not", "had not", "hasn't", "haven't", "hadn't"] [been, measured, shown, tested, run],    ["Limit:", "Limits:"],    ["not", "not been"] [measured, shown, tested, perceived],    ["not run", "not been run"],    [unmeasured, untested, unproven],    "yet to",    ["nothing runs", "nothing is measured"]  ],  KEEPS ["do not run", "to not run", "must not run", "should not run", "can not run", "will not run", "don't not run"],  IN [title, heading, lede, body, list],  LANGUAGE en,  CODE LACK_LISTED, SEVERITY error,  SAYS "The line lists a lack. State what the thing is and does, at its true size.",  REFUSED "No instrument here checks the perception: this was not yet measured.",  WRITTEN "The verifier checks the declaration.",  EVIDENCE "HOUSE-21; AWF-14; the author, 2026-11-08: Avoid this role of transparency that hurts the project image")

PROFILE: the measure of a voice

A PROFILE holds a text to bounds on its measures: the median sentence, the share of long and short sentences, and the marks per thousand words. Each measure outside its bound is one finding at level 4. The profile below belongs to the conformance suite.

DEFINE PROFILE voice (  SENTENCES_OVER 40 AT_MOST 10 PER_HUNDRED,  DASHES AT_MOST 4 PER_THOUSAND,  SENTENCE_MEDIAN 6 WITHIN 4,  MIN_WORDS 50,  EVIDENCE "The author, 2026-11-26: a ruling written for this fixture")

FORM: the structure of a page, a chapter or a post

A FORM places the title, the subject, the first sentence, the fixed headings and the close. The form governed_text belongs to the constitution, and the form page of the first house extends it with a title of six words at most.

DEFINE FORM governed_text (  TITLE_HOLDS subject,  FIRST_SENTENCE_HOLDS subject,  SAY_ONCE FIRST 150,  HOLES_CLOSED_AT [review, published],  EVIDENCE "zayn/doc/design/WRITING.md rules 1 and 2 (the author, 2026-11-05 and 2026-11-14); AYOUNI Writing Framework C4")
DEFINE FORM page (  EXTENDS governed_text,  TITLE_WORDS AT_MOST 6,  EVIDENCE "zayn/doc/design/WRITING.md rule 1, amended 2026-11-14 (ZAYN-SITE-RETHINK-01 ruling 3)")

ZONE: a region excused with a reason

A ZONE excuses a region of a text from the codes it names. The region opens in the text at the comment <!-- prosa:zone record --> and closes at <!-- prosa:end -->.

DEFINE ZONE record (  EXEMPT_FROM [LACK_LISTED],  REASON "A zone that quotes the project record")

ALLOW: one line excused with a reason

An ALLOW excuses one line of one file from one code. The excuse lives as long as the line: the court refuses, as ALLOW_STALE, an excuse that matches no finding.

DEFINE ALLOW quoted_record (  FILE "allow-lawful.md",  LINE_HOLDS "not yet signed",  EXCUSES LACK_LISTED,  REASON "The line quotes the record")

PIECE: a text bound to its style

A PIECE names one file or a set of files, the style they follow, their form and their subject term. The piece below governs the pages of this site: each page states its subject in its head, and the court asks for that term in the title and in the first sentence.

DEFINE PIECE site_pages (  FILES "*.html",  FOLLOWS ayouni_public,  FORM page,  SUBJECT_FROM front_matter)

READING: a question that stays with a reader

A READING names a judgment the court leaves to a reader, a person or an AI model: it states the question and the folder of records. The command prosa readings lists each reading as recorded or due.

DEFINE READING voice_reader (  ON chapter,  ASKS "Which three passages are most him, and which one is least him?",  RECORDS "research/voice")

The files the ten examples are cut from

Each example above is cut at build time from a file of the repository, so the page and the court read the same text.

  • The examples of STYLE, TERM, WORDLIST, PATTERN, FORM and READING come from styles/ayouni/ayouni.proz, the style of the first house.
  • The form governed_text comes from constitution/reader_rights.proz.
  • The examples of PROFILE, ZONE and ALLOW come from the conformance fixtures.
  • The example of PIECE comes from site/site.proz, the declaration of this site.
