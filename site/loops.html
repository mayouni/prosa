The two loops of Prosa

A loop of Prosa is the cycle in which a remark or a draft meets the court: the author's loop turns a remark into a rule, and the AI agent's loop turns a finding into a repair. The two loops ensure that the author rules once and that the AI agent meets each refusal at the moment of writing.

The author's loop: from a remark to a rule

  1. The author reads a draft and makes a remark.
  2. The AI agent writes the remark as a rule: the quote as EVIDENCE, the faulty sentence as REFUSED, the corrected one as WRITTEN.
  3. The court tests the rule against its own two examples at level 2.
  4. The author accepts or declines the rule, and the commit carries it.
  5. The court enforces the rule on every book and every site that follows the style.

The rule below, from the constitution, has this shape: its evidence cites the framework of the first house, and its two examples are its test.

DEFINE PATTERN typed_hole_filler (  MATCHES ["lorem ipsum", "TODO", "TBD", "XXX", "insert" WITHIN 3 "here"],  IN [title, heading, lede, body, list],  CODE PLACEHOLDER_LEFT, SEVERITY error,  SAYS "A placeholder stands where a fact belongs. Mark it [AUTHOR: what is needed] or state the fact.",  REFUSED "The study covered TBD participants.",  WRITTEN "The study covered [AUTHOR: number of participants] participants.",  EVIDENCE "AYOUNI Writing Framework C4: a hole is marked, it is never filled by an agent with a plausible particular")

The AI agent's loop: from a brief to a silent court

  1. The AI agent receives the contract of prosa brief before it drafts.
  2. The AI agent drafts the text.
  3. The AI agent runs prosa check --json and reads each finding as data.
  4. The AI agent repairs each finding by its code: the message shows the written form, and cites names the rule.
  5. The cycle ends when the court prints zero errors.

A reading of level 5 comes last, on a text already clean of what a machine decides.

$ prosa brief conformance/fixtures/term-synonym.proz --piece the_page# The writing contract of the piece the_pageThis contract states the declared style house for a text in language `en`. A finding names the rule, the written form and the ruling it comes from.

The Claude Code hook

The hook runs the court on every write to a governed file. The AI agent meets the refusal at the moment of writing, with the code and the written form, and repairs the line before it moves on.

The MCP surface

The MCP surface serves check, brief and measure to AI tools. An AI tool that speaks the protocol asks the court for a verdict, a contract or a profile as it asks any other tool.

What each party does in the two cycles

PartyWhat it does
The authorMakes a remark once, rules on each rule, and answers the readings.
The AI agentWrites rules from remarks, drafts under the brief, and repairs findings by code.
The courtRefuses by name what the declaration forbids, with the same verdict on every machine.
