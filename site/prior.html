Prior work compared with Prosa

The prior work of Prosa is the set of linters, controlled languages and studies that already govern or measure written text. This page lets a reader see what each one gives and what the new language contributes, with a link to each source.

The three things Prosa joins in one language

Each line of the table solves one part of the problem, and a house that wants all the parts runs several tools with several formats. Prosa joins three of them in one declaration that one court reads.

  • Prosa binds a rule to the ruling that created it: the EVIDENCE field is required, and the diagnostic cites it.
  • Prosa measures a draft against the profile of its own author: prosa measure takes the profile from approved pages.
  • Prosa declares what a text is about, so a rule of position becomes a test the court decides: the title names the subject term.

The three checkers of the first house, stated as one law

AYOUNI Press already carries its rules in three programs: a style lint for its books, a voice check for its public files and a shell check for a site. Prosa states the same rules in one language, and one court reads them. On 81 pages of three books in English and 42 pages in French, the twelve measures of the court equal those of the style lint on every page.
