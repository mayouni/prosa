# How to start the pump

This procedure starts the pump of the cooling circuit after a stop of more than one hour. It takes ten minutes and needs one technician.

## Before you start

- Make sure that the main switch is in the OFF position.
- Read the pressure on gauge G1. The pressure must be between 1.8 and 2.2 bar.
- Put on the gloves and the face shield.

## Procedure

1. Open the inlet valve fully. Turn the wheel to the left until it stops.
2. Open the outlet valve by two turns.
3. Set the main switch to ON. The green lamp comes on.
4. Push the START button for two seconds. The pump starts.
5. Wait for 30 seconds. Then read the pressure on gauge G2.
6. If the pressure is below 3 bar, stop the pump and close the two valves.
7. Open the outlet valve fully.

## After the procedure

Write the two pressures and the time in the log. Tell the shift supervisor that the circuit is in operation.
